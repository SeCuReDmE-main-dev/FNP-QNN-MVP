# Code Of Conduct

## Scope

This project is an educational, non-clinical, alpha-local research simulator.
Community interaction should stay constructive, evidence-based, and respectful.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and precise.
- Keep feedback actionable.
- Respect the non-clinical project boundary.
- Do not pressure maintainers to accept broad rewrites or unsafe claims.
- Do not request exposure of private CeLeBrUm material, secrets, or unpublished research text.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Medical, diagnostic, therapeutic, or safety-critical claims presented as fact.
- Attempts to publish private data, secrets, tokens, or unpublished private research.
- Unsolicited large rewrites that ignore `AGENTS.md`.

## Reporting

During the active crafting phase, use maintainer-guided discussion before opening
large issues or pull requests. Security-sensitive matters should follow
`SECURITY.md` and should not be disclosed publicly first.
