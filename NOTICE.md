Maintainer: Jean-Sébastien Beaulieu  
ORCID: https://orcid.org/0009-0007-2904-0443

## Project notice

This repository is an alpha-local, non-clinical educational/research simulator.
Its public use is limited to learning, code review, reproducible local experiments,
and non-clinical demonstration work.

## Required provenance and boundary

- Do not remove provenance notices in public documentation.
- Preserve the alpha-local and non-clinical boundary in README, docs, and published
  artifacts.
- Do not introduce clinical, diagnostic, therapeutic, emergency, safety-critical,
  or production healthcare claims.
- Do not commit secrets, private corpus text, private healthcare data, or private
  operational credentials in public outputs.

## Responsible reuse

This project is intentionally lightweight and research-oriented. Any derivative use
must preserve this boundary and keep human maintainer review for publication,
hosting, and legal release decisions.
